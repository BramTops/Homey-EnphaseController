Know where your sunshine goes, and decide what happens next. Enphase Controller turns your solar, battery and grid into a live picture in Homey, and lets you act on it by limiting production or exports when it matters. It also keeps an eye on your panels while you get on with your day: if one underperforms or goes quiet, Homey can tell you long before it shows up on your bill.

The Energy Flow widget even works with devices from other brands, so you can build the full picture of your home. Which features are available depends on your gateway and hardware.
