Integrate your Enphase Gateway (Envoy) for solar panel monitoring and control. Monitor live power production, daily and lifetime energy yields, and connected microinverters. Also adds the ability to turn off your solar array, disabling power production during negative energy prices.

Real-time monitoring works with any Enphase energy system, regardless of the account type. However, you need a linked Installer, Maintainer, or DIY/Self-Installer account to toggle the system on or off (it's free and easy to set up, check the community forum for detailed instructions).

NOTE: For unmetered Envoy gateways (Standard, or Metered without CT-clamps), a power ON or OFF command will take about 30 minutes to fully propagate to all the micro-inverters.
