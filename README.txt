Integrate your Enphase Gateway (Envoy) for solar panel monitoring and control. Monitor live power production, daily and lifetime energy yields, metered status, and individual microinverters. Track panel-level health and performance with automatic alerts for underperforming or offline solar panels. Also adds the ability to turn off your solar array, disabling power production during negative energy prices.

Real-time monitoring works with any Enphase energy system, regardless of the account type. However, you need a linked Installer, Maintainer, or DIY/Self-Installer account to toggle the system on or off. Note that for unmetered Envoy gateways, a power ON or OFF command will take about 30 minutes to fully propagate to all microinverters.
