Monitor solar production, grid import and export, home consumption, individual microinverters, and battery power and charge level of your Enphase systems. The Energy Flow widget animates live power between solar, grid, home, and battery. Battery monitoring is read-only; solar production can be controlled.

This app integrates well into the Energy tab and utilizes the latest Homey energy related features.

The widget automatically displays data from your Enphase system, including solar production, grid power and battery charge or discharge -- but you can also use Flow cards to supply values from other devices, so the widget works with any hardware combination. Home energy use is calculated automatically from the other readings, or you can set it directly with a Flow card. Configure the widget to customize its appearance and energy settings.

Monitoring and control features depend on gateway hardware, account access, and installed consumption clamps; the app will detect this automatically. See the community "Visit forum"-link below.
